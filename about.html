ABOUT THE PROJECT

An independent inquiry.

The Discharge Project is an independent research space at the intersection of theoretical physics, information, evolution, philosophy of mind, and artificial intelligence.

The purpose is not to force different sciences into one theory. It is to identify deep structural questions that appear when we move between matter, life, perception, knowledge, and intelligence.

The project is deliberately unfinished. Ideas are published as they develop, challenged by existing literature, and revised when evidence or argument requires it.
