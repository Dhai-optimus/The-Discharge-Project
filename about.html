ABOUT THE RESEARCHER

An independent inquiry.

I am an independent researcher working at the intersection of theoretical physics, philosophy, information, evolution, perception, and intelligence.

My work explores recurring questions that appear across matter, life, perception, knowledge, and intelligence. I am particularly interested in how physical systems acquire information, construct possibilities, and generate increasingly complex forms of organization and understanding.

My research is deliberately open-ended. Ideas are developed through writing, comparison with existing literature, conceptual analysis, and continual revision as arguments become clearer and new questions emerge.
