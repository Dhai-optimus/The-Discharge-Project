A WORKING PHILOSOPHY

The Framework

This is not a finished doctrine. It is a map of questions and provisional concepts that are being tested through writing, reading, and research.

DischargePerceptionLifeSpaceIntelligence
01

Discharge

Working idea: systems under constraint interact, transform differences, and arrive at configurations that temporarily stabilize the relation between them.

The term is deliberately provisional. The project asks whether a similar structural pattern can be recognized across thermodynamic processes, organism–environment relations, perception, and epistemic change without pretending that these domains are physically identical.

02

Perception as deletion

Perception may be understood not only as acquiring information, but as progressively eliminating alternatives that are irrelevant or incompatible with action.

On this view, an organism does not encounter an undifferentiated world and then simply copy it. It inherits and constructs constraints that make some possibilities salient and others effectively invisible.

03

Life as embodied knowledge

An organism can be viewed as a physical reservoir of historically successful solutions. Its structure embodies information about environments in which its lineage persisted.

This does not mean that organisms literally “contain propositions.” It is a philosophical hypothesis about how evolutionary history becomes embodied organization.

04

Space as a model for action

Instead of beginning with space as an empty container, ask what an embodied agent must model in order to move, avoid, reach, manipulate, and coordinate.

This line of thought connects the metaphysics of space with perception and action while remaining distinct from the claim that physical spacetime is merely subjective.

05

Intelligence

Intelligence is approached here as the capacity of a system to discover, preserve, transform, and deploy useful distinctions under constraint.

AI then becomes more than a technological field: it becomes a test case for whether intelligence can be instantiated in systems whose history, embodiment, and objectives differ radically from biological organisms.
