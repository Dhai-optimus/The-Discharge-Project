FEATURED RESEARCH

The Discharge Project

The Discharge Project explores whether processes of constraint, interaction, transformation, and stabilization can reveal a common structure across physical, biological, cognitive, and epistemic domains.

The project is still under development. Its aim is to develop a conceptual vocabulary for comparing physical, biological, cognitive, and epistemic processes without assuming that they are identical or reducible to one another.
